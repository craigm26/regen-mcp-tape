## What this run taught
A second TypeScript build, laid out differently (three modules, a separate fake server),
passed every case again. Its findings were about regular expressions, the part of the spec
most exposed to a second language: the anchors `^` and `$` were allowed in user patterns but
their meaning was never stated, and a sentence promising linear time for all input was
broader than anything the suite checks or the default patterns deliver. It also found a
place where my restatement of redaction and the earlier implementation quietly differ (user
patterns over already-replaced text), which became an open item rather than a rule.
